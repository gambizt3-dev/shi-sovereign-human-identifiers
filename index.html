Safety by design

Three separate security layers

LAYER 1

Savings vault

Your separate manual wallets. The AI Handler cannot withdraw from them.

LAYER 2

Human SHI

Your identity and spending wallet, protected by your own keys, recovery process and authorisation.

LAYER 3

AI Handler

A disposable, separately funded wallet with limited delegated permissions. Never give it your savings seed.

Security reality: cloud environment secrets are accessible to authorised running code. Keep unrestricted identity keys and main wallet seeds outside the hosted application. Revoking an AI delegation cannot stop someone who already stole that AI wallet's seed.

Human uniqueness, biometric privacy, zero-knowledge proofs and cross-chain verification remain research and development work — they are not implemented by this website.
